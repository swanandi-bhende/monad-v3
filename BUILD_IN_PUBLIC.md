## Project announcement: LinkedIn post (copy)

> I built Gig-a-Chad at Monad Blitz Pune V3.
>
> It's a work and evaluation marketplace on Monad Testnet. Creators post a task with a scoring rubric and fund it in USDC. Anyone can deliver. Reviewers score the work against the rubric and earn a reward for each useful review. The creator picks the winner, and the escrow pays out automatically.
>
> Two things make it different: a review marketplace where judging work is paid, and reputation split into builder and reviewer records, both built from on-chain activity.
